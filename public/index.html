How a room goes

  1. 01

    Open a room on the computer driving the projector or the TV.

  2. 02

    Show the QR. It sits at full size on the left until you hide it with Q.

  3. 03

    Everyone sends. Text, photos, links, drawings, emoji. Each arrival gets a few seconds at full size, then settles onto the board.

  4. 04

    Clear it for a fresh wall, or just close the tab and walk away.

No accounts

Nobody signs up and nobody installs anything. The QR is the whole onboarding.

Nothing saved

Rooms live in memory. Stop the server and the wall is genuinely gone.

Same Wi-Fi

It runs here. Phones reach it over the local network, with no cloud in between.
